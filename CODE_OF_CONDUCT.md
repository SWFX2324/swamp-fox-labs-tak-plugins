# Code of conduct

This project is for anyone who runs TAK and mesh radios, from first-time users to career professionals. Everyone should be able to ask a question, report a problem or suggest a change without being treated badly for it.

This code applies in the project's issues, pull requests and other GitHub spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## What's expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep criticism on the work, not the person.
- Help people who are new to TAK or mesh networking. Nobody has to know the jargon to take part.
- Back up technical claims with logs, a redacted snapshot or a field test where you can.
- Accept a decision or correction gracefully, even when you disagree.

## What's not acceptable

- Harassment, threats, insults or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background, rank, service, or level of experience.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission, including their location, their unit's, or their network details.
- Posting real operational details, or pressing anyone to share them.
- Repeated disruption, spam or trolling.

## Reporting a problem

Use the **Report** option in the **...** menu on the comment or issue, or [report abuse to GitHub](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse). To raise something less serious, you can mention the maintainer in a public comment instead. Please don't post anyone's private information in a report made in public.

Security vulnerabilities go through the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What happens next

The maintainer reviews reports and may edit or remove content, give a warning, or block someone from the project, depending on how serious the behavior is and whether it's repeated. The maintainer tries to treat everyone involved fairly, and won't retaliate against anyone for a report made in good faith.
